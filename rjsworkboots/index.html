Why RJ's

The Boot Shop That Still Knows How to Size a Foot

Big-box stores guess. Online retailers ship and pray. We measure, talk through the job you actually do, and pair you with the boot that will hold up. That's the only reason a specialty shop survives.

Expert Fitting

We measure your foot the right way, walk you through stretch points, and tell you which sock will keep you from blistering. Hard-to-fit feet are our specialty — most customers find us after their last pair didn't work.

Brands the Chains Don't Carry

White's, Nick's, Haix, Danner — the Pacific Northwest boot-makers serious tradespeople and wildland firefighters ask for by name. Stocked locally so you can try them on before you spend $400.

Built for the Job You Actually Do

Wildland fire. Union construction. Warehouse. Electrical. Oil & gas. We stock by hazard — composite toe, ESD, puncture-resistant, waterproof — and we ask what you do before we hand you a box.

Meet R + J

The Owners Are Behind the Counter

Ryan and Joseph opened RJ's in December 2020 with one idea: bring back the specialty boot shop. The kind where the owner measures your foot, asks what you do, and won't sell you the wrong boot just because it's on the shelf.

Five years in, both are still on the floor most days. You'll usually find Ryan sizing wildland firefighters in the back wall, or Joseph talking a union ironworker through whether he really needs a 10" or an 8". The names are right there on the door.

It's a small store. That's on purpose. Every boot on the wall earned its spot.
