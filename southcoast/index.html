Our Mission

A positive sports atmosphere, where every athlete reaches their potential.

For more than three decades, South Coast Aquatics has been a home for athletes in the Conejo Valley. We're built on a simple idea: when coaches encourage, athletes excel.

Our programs are designed to develop the whole athlete — technical skill, mental toughness, and the kind of sportsmanship that travels far beyond the pool.

  • ✓ Encouraging, certified coaching staff
  • ✓ Year-round development, no off-season gaps
  • ✓ Pathways from Splash Ball to 18U Elite
  • ✓ Strong, supportive Conejo Valley community
